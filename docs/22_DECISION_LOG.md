# 22. Decision Log

## Purpose

This document defines how architecture decisions will be recorded and governed. It ensures that important architectural choices remain visible, explainable, and traceable over time.

## Core Principles

- Architecture decisions must be documented.
- Every decision must explain the reasoning behind it.
- Alternatives must remain visible.
- Decisions must be linked to impact and future implications.
- Documentation must remain updated when decisions evolve.

## Decision Record Format

Each architecture decision should include:

### ID

A unique identifier for the decision.

### Date

The date on which the decision was recorded.

### Author

The person or role responsible for documenting the decision.

### Reason

The architectural or governance rationale behind the decision.

### Alternatives

The options considered before the final decision.

### Final Decision

The approved architectural decision.

### Impact

The expected architectural, governance, or organizational effect of the decision.

## Future Expansion

The decision log will grow as the platform evolves. Each new decision must strengthen consistency and architectural continuity.
