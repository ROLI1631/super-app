# 15. Project Roadmap

## Purpose

This document describes the high-level roadmap of the platform. It defines an ordered progression of architectural maturity and capability expansion without prescribing implementation detail.

## Core Principles

- The roadmap must preserve the platform’s architectural integrity.
- Each phase must strengthen the ecosystem rather than fragment it.
- Growth must remain modular, governed, and scalable.
- The roadmap reflects a progressive expansion from foundation to broader platform capability.

## Roadmap Phases

### Phase 1 — Architecture

Establish the architectural foundation, documentation system, governance principles, and structural coherence of the platform.

### Phase 2 — Identity

Develop the identity layer as the foundation for ownership, authentication, continuity, and trust.

### Phase 3 — User Cabinet

Establish the user-facing environment where identity, preferences, records, and personal platform relationships are organized.

### Phase 4 — Catalog

Introduce the catalog layer for structured offerings, discovery, and representation of platform content.

### Phase 5 — Marketplace

Expand into the marketplace layer where exchange, coordination, and participant interaction become central concerns.

### Phase 6 — Search

Provide platform-wide discovery and navigation capabilities that connect content, entities, and modules.

### Phase 7 — Communication

Extend the platform with structured communication functions for notifications, support, and internal interaction.

### Phase 8 — AI

Introduce AI capabilities in a governance-aware and modular manner that respects the platform’s Constitution and architecture.

### Phase 9 — Platform Expansion

Continue ecosystem growth through additional modules, integrations, and future capabilities while preserving the platform’s core architecture.

## Future Expansion

The roadmap is intended to guide disciplined growth. Future phases must remain aligned with the platform’s vision, modularity, and constitutional boundaries.
