# 36. Legal Core Specification

## Purpose

This document defines the Legal Core of the SO8FIREF platform. Legal Core is one of the permanent core layers of the ecosystem and provides the architectural basis for legal consent, evidence, governance continuity, and constitutional alignment across the platform. It does not replace legal responsibility or act as a legal representative.

Legal Core exists to preserve the platform’s legal boundaries as a technology operator and to ensure that platform modules remain consistent with the Constitution, consent records, and technical evidence of legal standing.

## 1. Purpose

### Purpose

To define why Legal Core exists and why it governs consent, record integrity, governance continuity, and constitutional alignment without becoming a legal participant.

### Responsibilities

- Preserve the platform’s constitutional and consent-based governance model.
- Maintain the architectural distinction between technical evidence and legal responsibility.
- Support legal continuity across identity, trust, communication, catalog, navigation, search, and future modules.
- Ensure that platform modules remain aligned with the platform’s non-representative role.

## 3. Core Objects

### Purpose

To define the conceptual objects that belong to Legal Core.

### Responsibilities

- Consent Record: the technical record of consent within the platform context.
- Governance Record: a preserved record of the platform’s governed standing.
- Evidence Record: the technical evidence supporting a governed action or relationship.
- Responsibility Boundary: the conceptual separation between platform operation and user legal responsibility.
- Future Legal Objects: reserved placeholders for additional legal concepts.

## 4. Relationships

### Purpose

To describe how Legal Core conceptually interacts with the broader architecture.

### Responsibilities

- Identity Core: legal continuity depends on identity continuity and ownership context.
- Trust Core: legal governance and trust remain conceptually distinct.
- Communication Core: communication may carry legal or consent-related context without replacing legal governance.
- Search Core: search may surface legal records without owning legal reasoning.
- Catalog Core: catalog structures may reference legal categories without owning legal logic.
- Time Core: temporal context supports legal continuity and historical record integrity.
- User Cabinet and Business Cabinet: each remains subject to legal boundaries rather than defining them.
