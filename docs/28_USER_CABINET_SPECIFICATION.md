# 28. User Cabinet Specification

## Purpose

This document defines the functional architecture of the User Cabinet within the SO8FIREF platform. The User Cabinet is the user’s personal domain inside the ecosystem, where identity, preferences, personal records, and participation across modules are organized under a coherent governance model.

## Core Principles

- Personal ownership is central to the User Cabinet.
- Privacy must be preserved throughout the user’s experience and record lifecycle.
- The User Cabinet must remain integrated with identity and trust.
- The User Cabinet must remain modular and scalable.
- The User Cabinet must support continuity across future platform modules.

## 1. Purpose

### Purpose

To define the role of the User Cabinet as the user’s primary domain of personal presence, control, and participation within the platform.

### Responsibilities

- Provide a coherent personal environment for the user within the ecosystem.
- Preserve the user’s relationship to identity, trust, privacy, and platform participation.
- Act as a personal coordination point for modules that are directly associated with the user.

## 2. Principles

### Purpose

To define the governing principles of the User Cabinet.

### Responsibilities

- Personal ownership: the user retains control over their personal environment and associated rights.
- Privacy: the cabinet must preserve confidentiality, minimize exposure, and separate sensitive information from general platform activity.
- Identity integration: the cabinet must remain aligned with the platform’s identity model.
- Modularity: the cabinet must support distinct capabilities as independent modules or sections.
- Scalability: the cabinet must support future expansion without collapsing into a single undifferentiated experience.

## 3. Cabinet Structure

### Purpose

To define the conceptual sections of the User Cabinet.

### Responsibilities

- Dashboard: the primary orientation area for the user’s personal platform situation and overview.
- Profile: the user’s structured personal identity representation within the platform.
- Avatar: the symbolic or visual representation associated with the user’s identity.
- Personal Data: the user’s personal records and personal information domain.
- Locations: the user’s location-related context and structured geographic information.
- Social: the user’s social presence and interaction-related domain.
- Search: the user’s personal discovery and navigation experience across platform content.
- P2P: the user’s peer-to-peer interaction domain.
- Exchange: the user’s exchange-related participation and coordination domain.
- Dating Agency: the user’s relationship-oriented interaction domain.
- Services: the user’s personal access to service-based offerings.
- Subscriptions: the user’s recurring participation and platform relationships.
- Future Modules: reserved areas for future cabinet capabilities and personal domain extensions.

## 4. Responsibilities

### Purpose

To define what the User Cabinet owns and what it does not own.

### Responsibilities

- The User Cabinet owns the user’s personal domain within the platform.
- The User Cabinet owns the user’s personal representation, preferences, and participation context.
- The User Cabinet owns the user’s relationship to their personal records and personal modules.
- The User Cabinet does not own the user’s legal identity outside the platform.
- The User Cabinet does not replace the platform’s role as operator.
- The User Cabinet does not become a participant in user-to-user agreements.
- The User Cabinet does not absorb unrelated organizational or marketplace governance responsibilities.

## 5. Relationships

### Purpose

To describe how the User Cabinet relates to the broader platform architecture.

### Responsibilities

- Identity: the cabinet is grounded in the platform’s identity model and user continuity.
- Trust: the cabinet participates in trust formation through identity, reputation, and personal standing.
- Communication: the cabinet connects the user to internal communication flows and support.
- Marketplace: the cabinet provides the personal perspective for participation in marketplace-related contexts.
- Search: the cabinet supports personal discovery and access to relevant content and relationships.
- Organizations: the cabinet may relate to formal or structured organizations through the user’s participation.
- Administration: the cabinet remains subject to governance and oversight within the broader platform.
- AI: the cabinet may interact with AI-driven assistance in a governed and privacy-conscious way.
