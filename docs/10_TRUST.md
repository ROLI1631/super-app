# 10. Trust

## Purpose

This document describes trust as a platform layer. Trust is treated as a structural quality that underpins identity, interaction, reputation, and long-term adoption within the ecosystem.

## Core Principles

- Trust must be built into the platform’s architecture and governance.
- Trust is not reduced to a score or a single metric.
- Trust emerges from identity integrity, privacy discipline, and clear boundaries of responsibility.
- Different forms of trust must be recognized, including identity trust, seller trust, service trust, and organization trust.

## Identity Trust

Identity trust is the confidence that a digital identity is legitimate, controlled, and appropriately represented. It is the basis for secure participation across modules.

## Seller Trust

Seller trust concerns the confidence that a participant behaves consistently and responsibly within the marketplace context. It depends on identity integrity, legal clarity, and platform governance.

## Service Trust

Service trust concerns the reliability and legitimacy of capabilities offered within the platform. It depends on consistent architectural behavior and clear boundaries of responsibility.

## Organization Trust

Organization trust concerns the confidence that institutions or groups are represented and governed in a way that preserves accountability and continuity.

## Verification Philosophy

Verification is an architectural and governance concept. It must be coherent, explainable, and aligned with the platform’s constitutional role. It should strengthen trust without creating excessive dependence on centralized judgment.

## Reputation Philosophy

Reputation is a signal of accumulated trustworthiness over time. It must not become an opaque substitute for identity or governance. It should remain understandable, fair, and structurally aligned with the platform’s principles.

## Future Expansion

Trust will become more complex as the platform grows across sectors, communities, and forms of interaction. New trust mechanisms must preserve the platform’s foundational principles rather than bypass them.
