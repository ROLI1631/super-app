# 12. Communication

## Purpose

This document describes the architecture of internal communication within the platform. Communication is treated as a core ecosystem layer that supports coordination, notification, support, and interaction.

## Core Principles

- Communication must remain internal to the platform environment.
- The platform must not rely on public email infrastructure for native communication.
- Internal messaging, notifications, and support must remain within the platform’s controlled architecture.
- Communication must remain modular and extensible.

## Internal Messaging

Internal messaging provides a structured channel for interaction within the ecosystem. It is part of the platform’s core architecture rather than a peripheral feature.

## Notifications

Notifications provide timely awareness of relevant events, changes, and actions. They must remain consistent with the platform’s governance and privacy principles.

## Support

Support communication is treated as an internal platform function. It must preserve the platform’s boundaries, privacy posture, and controlled communication model.

## Future Communication Modules

Future communication capabilities must extend the platform’s internal architecture without introducing external communication dependencies that contradict the Constitution.

## Future Expansion

Communication will continue to grow as a platform layer. New communication capabilities must remain modular, secure, and consistent with the platform’s internal model.
