# 07. Glossary

## Purpose

This document defines the official terminology of the SO8FIREF platform. It establishes a shared vocabulary for architecture, governance, identity, trust, privacy, and platform growth.

## Core Principles

- Terminology must remain consistent across all architecture documentation.
- Every defined term must support the platform’s long-term governance model.
- Definitions must remain conceptual and architectural, not implementation-specific.

## Glossary Terms

### SO8FIREF

- Definition: The product vision owner and architectural steward of the platform.
- Purpose: To preserve the platform’s strategic identity, governance direction, and long-term coherence.
- Related documents: [00_VISION.md](00_VISION.md), [01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md](01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md), [15_PROJECT_ROADMAP.md](15_PROJECT_ROADMAP.md)

### Platform

- Definition: The complete digital ecosystem governed by the Constitution and architectural framework.
- Purpose: To provide a modular environment for identity, interaction, exchange, communication, and intelligent coordination.
- Related documents: [00_VISION.md](00_VISION.md), [04_ARCHITECTURE.md](04_ARCHITECTURE.md)

### Operator

- Definition: The technical entity responsible for operating the platform infrastructure.
- Purpose: To provide technical services without becoming a participant in user-to-user legal relationships.
- Related documents: [01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md](01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md), [08_PLATFORM_PRINCIPLES.md](08_PLATFORM_PRINCIPLES.md)

### Identity

- Definition: The recognized digital presence of a person, organization, or entity within the platform.
- Purpose: To establish continuity, ownership, trust, and accountability across modules.
- Related documents: [02_NUMERIC_LANGUAGE.md](02_NUMERIC_LANGUAGE.md), [09_IDENTITY.md](09_IDENTITY.md)

### Digital Identity

- Definition: The platform-recognized representation of a person or entity in digital form.
- Purpose: To support access, ownership, interaction, and trust without requiring physical presence.
- Related documents: [09_IDENTITY.md](09_IDENTITY.md), [10_TRUST.md](10_TRUST.md)

### Recovery Key

- Definition: The controlled secret that enables the owner to preserve access to their account and digital rights.
- Purpose: To preserve user ownership and prevent platform-mediated recovery from undermining the user’s independence.
- Related documents: [01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md](01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md), [09_IDENTITY.md](09_IDENTITY.md)

### Passkey

- Definition: The authentication method defined by the platform’s constitutional model.
- Purpose: To provide secure authentication while preserving the principle of zero biometric storage.
- Related documents: [01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md](01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md), [09_IDENTITY.md](09_IDENTITY.md)

### Trust

- Definition: The confidence that the platform, its actors, and its systems are legitimate, reliable, and aligned with their stated role.
- Purpose: To support safe interaction, reputation, and long-term adoption.
- Related documents: [10_TRUST.md](10_TRUST.md), [11_PRIVACY.md](11_PRIVACY.md)

### Privacy

- Definition: The principle that personal and sensitive information should be minimized, protected, and controlled by the user.
- Purpose: To preserve dignity, reduce exposure, and maintain confidence in the platform.
- Related documents: [01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md](01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md), [11_PRIVACY.md](11_PRIVACY.md)

### Module

- Definition: A discrete, independently maintainable capability within the platform.
- Purpose: To preserve modular growth, replaceability, and loose coupling.
- Related documents: [03_MODULARITY.md](03_MODULARITY.md), [14_PLATFORM_MODULES.md](14_PLATFORM_MODULES.md)

### Entity

- Definition: Any identifiable element within the platform’s conceptual model.
- Purpose: To ensure consistent treatment of users, services, products, messages, records, and other platform elements.
- Related documents: [02_NUMERIC_LANGUAGE.md](02_NUMERIC_LANGUAGE.md), [04_ARCHITECTURE.md](04_ARCHITECTURE.md)

### Numeric Language

- Definition: The architectural concept of representing platform entities through a universal numeric identity framework.
- Purpose: To create coherence across modules and preserve conceptual consistency.
- Related documents: [02_NUMERIC_LANGUAGE.md](02_NUMERIC_LANGUAGE.md), [04_ARCHITECTURE.md](04_ARCHITECTURE.md)

### Architecture

- Definition: The structured framework that organizes the platform’s layers, modules, principles, and governance model.
- Purpose: To ensure scalability, clarity, and long-term maintainability.
- Related documents: [04_ARCHITECTURE.md](04_ARCHITECTURE.md), [05_DEVELOPMENT_RULES.md](05_DEVELOPMENT_RULES.md)

### Constitution

- Definition: The highest authority governing the platform’s legal, security, privacy, and architectural principles.
- Purpose: To prevent deviations from the platform’s core commitments.
- Related documents: [01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md](01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md), [08_PLATFORM_PRINCIPLES.md](08_PLATFORM_PRINCIPLES.md)

### Vision

- Definition: The long-term purpose and reason for the platform’s existence.
- Purpose: To provide direction for growth and strategic coherence.
- Related documents: [00_VISION.md](00_VISION.md), [15_PROJECT_ROADMAP.md](15_PROJECT_ROADMAP.md)

### Immutable Record

- Definition: A permanent legal or technical record that is preserved in a stable and non-reversible form.
- Purpose: To support accountability, evidence, and legal integrity.
- Related documents: [01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md](01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md), [13_DATA_LIFECYCLE.md](13_DATA_LIFECYCLE.md)

### Internal Communication

- Definition: The platform-native communication system used for notifications, support, and ecosystem coordination.
- Purpose: To keep communication within the platform’s controlled environment.
- Related documents: [01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md](01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md), [12_COMMUNICATION.md](12_COMMUNICATION.md)

### Technical Operator

- Definition: The organizational or technical role responsible for operating the platform’s infrastructure.
- Purpose: To uphold the platform’s technical continuity while preserving the constitutional boundary between operator and participant.
- Related documents: [01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md](01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md), [08_PLATFORM_PRINCIPLES.md](08_PLATFORM_PRINCIPLES.md)

### Legal Consent

- Definition: A recorded confirmation that a user has accepted the required legal conditions and responsibilities.
- Purpose: To preserve evidence of user understanding and legal accountability.
- Related documents: [01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md](01_SYSTEM_CONSTITUTION.md), [10_TRUST.md](10_TRUST.md)

### Future Expansion

- Definition: The planned growth of the platform through new capabilities and modules.
- Purpose: To ensure that growth remains modular, governed, and respectful of the existing architecture.
- Related documents: [00_VISION.md](00_VISION.md), [15_PROJECT_ROADMAP.md](15_PROJECT_ROADMAP.md)

## Future Expansion

The glossary will evolve as the platform grows. New terms must be introduced only when they strengthen architectural clarity and shared understanding.
