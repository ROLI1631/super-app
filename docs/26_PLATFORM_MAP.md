# 26. Platform Map

## Purpose

This document serves as the master conceptual map of the SO8FIREF platform. It describes the platform as a modular digital ecosystem and shows how its major areas relate to one another without prescribing implementation detail.

## Core Principles

- The platform is a modular digital ecosystem rather than a single-purpose website.
- Every major area of the platform has a defined architectural purpose.
- The platform grows through layered responsibilities and modular extension.
- The map is conceptual, governance-oriented, and technology-independent.

## 1. Platform Overview

### Purpose

To define the platform as a unified ecosystem composed of identity, trust, communication, discovery, exchange, governance, and expansion capabilities.

### Responsibilities

- Provide a coherent environment for users, organizations, services, and offerings.
- Preserve the platform’s constitutional role as an infrastructure and governance layer.
- Support continuity across identity, communication, navigation, and interaction.
- Maintain a long-term structure for growth and modular extension.

## 2. Core Layers

### Purpose

To identify the major architectural layers that give the platform its structure and coherence.

### Responsibilities

- Identity Core: preserve digital presence, ownership continuity, and recognized participation.
- Authentication Core: uphold secure access and controlled identity recognition.
- Trust Core: preserve confidence in parties, services, organizations, and interactions.
- Legal Core: maintain the platform’s constitutional and consent-based governance model.
- Privacy Core: protect user control, minimize exposure, and preserve confidentiality.
- Communication Core: govern internal messaging, notifications, support, and coordination.
- Navigation Core: support orientation, discovery, and movement through the ecosystem.
- Search Core: enable structured discovery across the platform’s content and entities.
- Catalog Core: organize offerings and structured representations for discovery and reference.
- Marketplace Core: support exchange, coordination, and interaction across participants and offerings.
- Analytics Core: provide disciplined observation and reporting without undermining privacy.
- Geo Core: support location-based organization and spatial awareness.
- Time Core: support temporal structure, scheduling, continuity, and lifecycle coordination.
- Notification Core: provide awareness of relevant events and platform actions.
- Organization Core: represent formal or structured groups and collective participation.
- Administration Core: preserve oversight, governance, and platform stewardship.

## 3. Platform Modules

### Purpose

To describe the major platform modules that fulfill the ecosystem’s core capabilities.

### Responsibilities

- User Cabinet: provide the personal environment for identity, preferences, and user-oriented coordination.
- Business Cabinet: support participant-facing activity related to services, offerings, and professional presence.
- Administrator Cabinet: provide governance-oriented oversight and platform administration.
- Organization Cabinet: support collective identity and structured community or organizational participation.
- Marketplace: provide the conceptual context for exchange and interaction between parties and offerings.
- Products: structure and represent goods or tangible offerings.
- Services: structure and represent service-based offerings.
- Rent: support arrangements involving temporary use or access.
- Exchange: support coordinated transfer or participation between parties.
- P2P: support direct peer-to-peer interaction patterns.
- Dating Agency: support relationship-oriented interactions within the platform context.
- Subscriptions: support recurring participation and continuous platform relationships.
- Documents: organize records, files, legal consent artifacts, and supporting documents.
- Support: provide a structured support and assistance domain.
- AI: provide intelligent assistance and adaptive guidance within governance boundaries.

## 4. Relationships

### Purpose

To explain how core layers support the platform’s modules and how the ecosystem remains coherent across domains.

### Responsibilities

- Identity Core supports user, organization, and participant identity across all relevant modules.
- Trust Core supports marketplace, services, organization, and interaction-oriented modules.
- Privacy Core supports documents, communication, identity, and any module handling sensitive information.
- Communication Core supports support, notifications, administration, and user coordination.
- Search Core supports discovery across catalog, marketplace, documents, and other content-bearing modules.
- Navigation Core supports movement between cabinets, modules, and platform domains.
- Analytics Core supports observation and reporting across modules without displacing privacy boundaries.
- Legal Core supports consent, records, governance, and trust-related modules.
