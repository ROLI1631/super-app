# 04. Platform Architecture

## Purpose

This document provides a high-level description of the platform architecture. It defines the major layers of the ecosystem and their responsibilities without prescribing implementation details.

## Core Principles

### 1. Vision

The vision defines the purpose of the platform and the long-term role it serves in the digital ecosystem.

### 2. Constitution

The Constitution governs all architectural decisions and ensures that the platform remains lawful, secure, modular, and trustworthy.

### 3. Identity

Identity provides the foundation for digital presence, authentication, legal consent, and continuity of user ownership across the ecosystem.

### 4. User Cabinet

The User Cabinet contains the user’s personal space, preferences, records, and relationship with the platform’s services.

### 5. Catalog

The Catalog organizes digital offerings, representations, and structured content that can be discovered and referenced across the platform.

### 6. Marketplace

The Marketplace enables exchange and coordination between users and offerings while preserving the platform’s role as infrastructure rather than as a direct participant in transactions.

### 7. Communication

Communication provides the internal channels for interaction, notifications, support, and ecosystem coordination.

### 8. AI

AI supports assistance, reasoning, navigation, and adaptive guidance while remaining governed by the Constitution and modular boundaries.

### 9. Extensions

Extensions provide the means for growth, customization, and ecosystem expansion without compromising the platform’s core architecture.

## Architectural Intent

The platform is structured as a layered ecosystem: each layer has a distinctive responsibility and contributes to a coherent whole. The goal is not to create a monolithic application, but a durable architecture that can expand over time without breaking its foundations.

## Future Expansion Notes

New capabilities must be introduced as extensions or modules that align with the existing architecture. The platform grows by extending layers in a disciplined way rather than by collapsing them into one indistinct system.
