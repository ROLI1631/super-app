# 35. Trust Core Specification

## Purpose

This document defines the Trust Core of the SO8FIREF platform. Trust Core is one of the permanent core layers of the ecosystem and provides the conceptual basis for trust relationships across identity, communication, marketplace, search, administration, and future modules.

## Core Principles

- Trust exists because the platform must support meaningful decisions across participants and capabilities.
- Trust is independent from Identity and must not be reduced to identity alone.
- Trust is reusable across all modules and domains.
- Trust is architectural, conceptual, and governance-oriented.
- Trust does not replace legal responsibility, identity, or platform authority.

## 3. Trust Objects

### Purpose

To describe the conceptual trust objects that belong to Trust Core.

### Responsibilities

- User Trust: the trust standing associated with an individual participant.
- Business Trust: the trust standing associated with an economic or commercial participant.
- Organization Trust: the trust standing associated with a collective or institutional participant.
- Service Trust: the trust standing associated with a platform capability or offered service.
- Product Trust: the trust standing associated with a product or offering.
- Document Trust: the trust standing associated with a formal or informational record.
- Transaction Trust: the trust standing associated with a specific interaction or exchange context.
- Communication Trust: the trust standing associated with an exchange of communication within the ecosystem.
- AI Trust: the trust standing associated with AI-assisted participation or decision support.
- Future Trust Objects: reserved conceptual space for additional trust categories as the platform evolves.

## 4. Trust Relationships

### Purpose

To explain how Trust Core conceptually interacts with the broader platform architecture.

### Responsibilities

- Identity Core: trust depends on identity continuity but remains conceptually separate from it.
- Legal Core: trust supports decision-making, but it never replaces legal responsibility.
- Communication Core: trust influences the standing of communication and interaction within the ecosystem.
- Marketplace: trust supports the evaluation of offerings, participants, and exchange contexts.
- Search: trust contributes to how entities and offerings are interpreted and prioritized.
- User Cabinet: trust influences the user’s personal experience and relationships within the platform.
- Business Cabinet: trust supports the standing of business participants within their ecosystem context.
- Organization Cabinet: trust supports the standing of organizations within their governed context.
- Administration: trust provides architectural context for oversight, governance, and continuity.
